Privacy policy

Local by design.

Last updated July 19, 2026

TabScroll does not send browsing data, URLs, screenshots, or preferences to any external service. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party telemetry.

Data TabScroll processes

  • Basic current-window tab state needed for switching, including identifiers, positions, and active state.
  • If you enable the optional tab-details permission: titles, open-tab addresses, and favicons for the current window.
  • A screenshot preview of the currently active tab when you explicitly open TabScroll.
  • A local light or night theme preference and onboarding-complete flag stored on your device.

How the data is used

  • To show the current window's tabs inside the TabScroll interface.
  • To let you navigate and activate the tab you choose.
  • To show the current-page preview and open-tab identity cards.
  • To remember your theme choice.

Storage and retention

Open-tab details and the current-tab screenshot are processed locally for the active overlay session. The screenshot is held only in memory as long as needed to render that session. Theme and onboarding preferences remain locally on your device until you change them or clear extension data. TabScroll does not create a browsing-history database.

Permissions

  • activeTab: provides temporary access after you explicitly invoke TabScroll, allowing the current-page preview and overlay injection.
  • scripting: injects the TabScroll overlay into the current page.
  • tabs (optional): reads the titles, addresses, favicons, order, and active state of tabs currently open in the window. The switcher remains usable if you decline.

What TabScroll does not do

  • It does not capture background-tab screenshots or request Chrome debugger access.
  • It does not sell, transfer, or share browsing data or screenshots.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or unrelated purposes.
  • It does not store a remote copy of your browsing activity.

Changes

If TabScroll's permissions, functionality, or data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the related release is published.
