**Alt-Tab for Chrome tabs: preview crowded windows and switch with your mouse wheel, arrow keys, or one shortcut.**

## Detailed description

Too many Chrome tabs to recognize?

TabScroll turns your current window into a full-screen visual tab switcher. Press `Ctrl+Shift+K` (`Command+Shift+K` on macOS), move through your open tabs with the mouse wheel or arrow keys, and press `Enter` to switch.

### See the tab before you switch

TabScroll shows each tab's title, URL, favicon, and visual preview when Chrome allows it. The centered card makes the selected tab clear, even when the browser tab strip has become a row of tiny icons.

### Fast controls

- Mouse wheel: move through tabs
- Arrow keys: previous or next tab
- Enter or click: activate the selected tab
- Escape: close the switcher
- T: switch between light and night themes

### Made for crowded browser windows

TabScroll is useful for developers, researchers, students, recruiters, shoppers, content creators, and anyone who regularly keeps many tabs open.

### Local by design

TabScroll has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party telemetry. Tab metadata and preview screenshots are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to an external service.

### Why Chrome shows a debugger message

To capture previews of eligible background tabs without visibly switching to each one, TabScroll temporarily uses Chrome's debugger interface while you explicitly use the extension. Chrome may display its native debugging banner during this process. TabScroll detaches after each capture and keeps the preview only in memory for the current switcher session.

Chrome internal pages such as `chrome://` pages and some Chrome Web Store pages do not allow extension overlays. Protected pages and unavailable previews use a visual fallback.
